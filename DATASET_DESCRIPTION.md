The study uses IVG-Bench, a repository-provided suite of 105 interactive cases across three simulated environments: CookSim (50 cooking cases spanning five recipe tiers and two kitchen layouts), VH-Home (25 household cases, with 17 defined tasks across five VirtualHome apartments), and ScreenSim (30 phone-interface cases based on 11 benchmark tasks). Each case specifies a goal and scheduled user events, such as mistakes, questions, or requests. Three simulated-user personas are evaluated per case, yielding 315 episodes per assistant per run: 150 cooking, 75 household, and 90 phone-interface episodes. The simulator generates visual observations, dialogue, actions, and evaluation records during each run; the repository supplies case definitions and supporting assets rather than a fixed corpus of prerecorded episodes. ScreenSim also contains a separate, disjoint training library of 67 tasks and 168 designed episodes; its 11 benchmark tasks are held out. VirtualHome and third-party visual assets remain subject to their upstream terms.
